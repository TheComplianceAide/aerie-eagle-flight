The 2019 Geological Society of America Penrose field guide describes interbedded metavolcanic rock and calcareous greywacke at the Mendenhall Glacier Visitor Center. Glacial abrasion exposes stretched layers, joints and fracture surfaces. The forms here represent worn, asymmetric bedrock, jointed blocks, and mixed loose sediment along the nearby shore. Individual stone positions, lithologies, joint orientations and minor beach bars are inferred. Vegetated banks, mixed sediment and surface tints vary across these parts of the foreland.

The pilot contains 28 worn bedrock exposures, 98 jointed blocks and 845 small clasts. Shapes are reconstructed in three morphology families with distinct profiles, jointing, burial and face normals. The 15 spatial batches contain 44,345 triangles. Substantial surfaces use their rendered upper triangles for flight clearance. Smaller clasts share ground clearance. Habitat and local perch rendering retain their existing roots and footholds.
