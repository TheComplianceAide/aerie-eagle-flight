AERIE · REGIONAL LAND WILDLIFE

These are representative encounters placed on the game's terrain. The animal
species are documented in the relevant regions. Exact animals, group sizes,
sightings, movement, times and approach positions are artistic simulations.

SPECIES AND HABITATS

Moosehead Lake, Maine
Maine Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry, Moosehead Region
Management Plan. The Little Moose Unit supports moose, white-tailed deer and
snowshoe hare. Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife describes
moose proportions, antlers, neck bell and feeding at wet forest margins.
The snowshoe hare wears its brown summer coat in the game.
https://www.maine.gov/dacf/parks/get_involved/planning_and_acquisition/management_plans/docs/MooseheadMgmtPlan-FINAL%20DRAFT_4-7-16-part1.pdf
https://www.maine.gov/ifw/fish-wildlife/wildlife/species-information/mammals/moose.html
https://www.maine.gov/ifw/fish-wildlife/wildlife/species-information/mammals/cottontail-snowshoe-hare.html

Lake Winnipesaukee, Surry Mountain Lake and Dixville Notch, New Hampshire
New Hampshire Fish and Game documents white-tailed deer, moose and snowshoe
hare in New Hampshire. Moose are featured at the northern Dixville site. Deer
and snowshoe hare occupy forest margins in the two more southerly lake worlds.
https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt746/files/inline-documents/sonh/nh-deer-assessment-2015.pdf
https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt746/files/inline-documents/sonh/nh-snowshoe-hare-assessment-2015.pdf

Lac Berté, Côte-Nord, Québec
Québec's regional forest plan lists moose and snowshoe hare in the Côte-Nord.
https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/forets/documents/planification/Cote-Nord/plans/DS_Territoire_PAFIT_R09_MRNF.pdf
https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/faune/animaux-sauvages-quebec/fiches-especes-fauniques/orignal
https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/faune/animaux-sauvages-quebec/fiches-especes-fauniques/lievre-amerique

Mendenhall Glacier, Alaska
Alaska Department of Fish and Game describes American black bears drawn to
salmon at Mendenhall's Steep Creek. The game encounter follows a lake bank.
https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=southeastviewing.juneaumendenhallglacier

Horseshoe Bend, Arizona
National Park Service, Glen Canyon National Recreation Area. Desert bighorn
sheep use rocky benches in the canyons. Desert cottontails feed near cover.
https://www.nps.gov/glca/learn/nature/mammals.htm
Local desert cottontail observation near Page:
https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/406861108

Yosemite Valley, California
National Park Service documents mule deer browsing in the valley meadows.
https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/mammals.htm
https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/meadows.htm

Crater Lake, Oregon
National Park Service lists black-tailed deer, mule deer and American pikas.
The Rim route uses a Columbian black-tailed deer group.
https://www.nps.gov/crla/learn/nature/mammals.htm

Oeschinensee, Switzerland
Canton of Bern Hunting Inspectorate, ibex colony monitoring. The local park
operator identifies sensitive alpine wildlife refuges around Oeschinensee.
https://www.weu.be.ch/de/start/themen/jagd-fischerei/jagd-wildtiere/zahlen-fakten-jagd.html
https://www.oeschinensee.ch/en/wandern/
Local ibex observation in Kandersteg:
https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/408850841

Reinefjorden, Lofoten, Norway
Lofotodden National Park documents Eurasian otter on Lofoten's coast.
https://www.lofotoddennp.com/en/vegetation-and-wildlife/

Milford Sound, New Zealand
New Zealand Department of Conservation records introduced red deer throughout
Fiordland. Existing fur seals and native birds use their separate habitats.
https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/things-to-do/hunting/where-to-hunt/fiordland/fiordland-hunting/what-to-hunt/

MODELS AND MATERIALS

Mammal bodies, face markings, distinct antlers and horns, limb articulation,
movement and spatial placement are original game models. The shared close fur
detail in wild-mammal-coat.webp is derived from Bicanski's photograph “fur,
rabbit,” CC0 1.0. It was cropped, rotated, desaturated, high-pass filtered and
resized. Anatomical markings and colour differ by species.
https://pixnio.com/textures-and-patterns/fur/fur-of-rabbit
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The Lofoten otter's wave-washed ledge is original relief with the game's
existing photographed shore-stone material, attributed in credits.txt.
