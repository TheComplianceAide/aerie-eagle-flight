Lac Berté — Côte-Nord, Québec, Canada
South basin and Camp Sud

The playable terrain covers a 9.6 km square centered at 50.747413 N, 68.500225 W. The distant terrain covers 36 km. The engine uses local metre coordinates in WGS 84 / UTM zone 19N. The flight boundary is inset from the high-detail terrain edge.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023 and 2024. True-colour imagery, vegetation classes, lake outlines, island boundaries, terrain colours and littoral distance were derived from Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery. The mosaic uses the August 29, 2024 acquisition first, with classified clear pixels from July 7 and 12, 2024, and September 4, 2023 filling gaps. Visible imagery is 10 m per pixel at source; scene classification is 20 m. Processed November 19, 2026. Source granules are listed below.

The operator identifies Camp Sud on a point facing a narrow channel carrying a current. The present-day roof appears in independent summer 2015 and 2022 orthophotos near 50.73166° N, 68.50079° W. The game follows that site and its lake-facing orientation. The small raised cabin, porch, peeled timber steps and skiff represent the earlier family photographs. Their dimensions, the original camera and the precise historical mooring are interpretations. The rain shower and rainbow portray passing weather.

Camp Sud field data © Gouvernement du Québec, ministère des Ressources naturelles et des Forêts, CC BY 4.0. Adapted the 2015 30 cm and 2022 Côte-Nord 15 cm natural-colour airborne imagery and the 1 m bare-earth and canopy-height models on sheets 22K10NE and 22K09NO. Reprojected the images and height fields; traced the wet/dry banks, estimated shallow-water transmission and relief from the 2015 pixels, reconstructed the timbered clearing and derived crown positions and heights. The terrain grid retained the pitch of the present roof. Ground beneath its footprint was interpolated from surrounding returns to support the historical cabin. The low-water channel and relative depth are optical estimates. Water velocity, species, lower branches and individual crown widths are approximations. Regional camp coverage is bounded; the wider lake retains the regional sources above.
