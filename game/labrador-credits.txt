Lac Berté — Côte-Nord, Québec, Canada
South basin and Camp Sud

The playable terrain covers a 9.6 km square centered at 50.747413 N, 68.500225 W. The distant terrain covers 36 km. The engine uses local metre coordinates in WGS 84 / UTM zone 19N. The flight boundary is inset from the high-detail terrain edge.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023 and 2024. True-colour imagery, vegetation classes, lake outlines, island boundaries, terrain colours and littoral distance were derived from Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery. The mosaic uses the August 29, 2024 acquisition first, with classified clear pixels from July 7 and 12, 2024, and September 4, 2023 filling gaps. Visible imagery is 10 m per pixel at source; scene classification is 20 m. Processed November 19, 2026. Source granules are listed below.

The operator's marked Camp Sud point was aligned with the lake outline to choose the headland. The cabin, landing, shoreline grading, underwater slope and tree placements are artistic reconstructions. The raised porch, peeled timber steps, exposed root and pale sandy landing were modeled from family reference photos. The headland surface and its tree roots follow the local rendered relief. The location of the family camera and the cabin footprint remain approximate. The interactive weather illustrates late-day conditions.
