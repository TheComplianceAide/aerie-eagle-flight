AERIE FIELD RECORDINGS

The eagle's voice is drawn from three recordings of bald eagles. The environmental mixes combine recordings from the depicted region with field recordings of the same habitat or species elsewhere. Recording locations and rights are listed below. The individual MP3 excerpts have been trimmed, resampled, level-adjusted and faded. Ambient excerpts have crossfaded loop seams.
