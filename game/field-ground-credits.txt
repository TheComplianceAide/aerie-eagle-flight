REGIONAL GROUND PHOTOGRAPHY
The field-ground-scans atlas combines diffuse color, ambient occlusion and measured displacement from these CC0 photogrammetry assets. They are material analogues for the simulated habitats. The geographic pattern and terrain color come from each region’s mapped terrain, aerial photograph and canopy survey.

Table Rock, Dixville Notch. USGS/FEMA 2015–2016 airborne lidar ground returns, sampled at 0.5 m in UTM zone 19N, twelve 128 m footprints. Overhead color is NH GRANIT six-inch 2021/2022 orthophotography resampled to 0.2 m. The local ground is blended into the existing survey over eight metres at the outside boundary.
