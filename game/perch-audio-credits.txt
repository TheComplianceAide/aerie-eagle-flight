AERIE PERCH CONTACT AND CANOPY RECORDINGS

Short Foley analogues in the game's spatial sound field. All clips were trimmed, folded to mono when necessary, filtered, level-matched, and faded. They are material analogues for simulated eagle contact.

perch-creak-a.m4a and perch-creak-b.m4a: Department64, "tree_creak.wav", from inside a tree. Based on the CC0 cleanup by AntumDeluge. Excerpts at 1.13–1.90 and 2.21–2.66 seconds of that edition.
https://freesound.org/people/Department64/sounds/95259/
