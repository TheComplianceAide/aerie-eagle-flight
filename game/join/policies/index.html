Ticket terms

Fly Lake Winnipesaukee free in your browser. You can take off without an email address.

The gold ticket is a one-time digital purchase from The ComplianceAide. It opens all Aerie worlds for you and includes one gift ticket for a friend. Stripe shows the total in US dollars, including applicable tax, before you pay. A checkout marked “test” simulates the purchase.

Once payment is confirmed, select Play now to fly. Open My ticket for your receipt and private, single-use gift link. Send that link directly to the person you want to have it. On a new device, select Restore ticket and use your checkout email.

The silver ticket opens five selected worlds. Share the game privately with five friends and confirm you did so, or submit a public post for review. Give your honest opinion and mention the worlds you receive. The gift copy comes with the gold ticket.

Refunds

If you were charged twice, bought a ticket by mistake, or cannot play what you bought, request a refund. Include your checkout email and what happened. We review each request and reply by email. Keep the case number from the form.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. A full refund closes the paid access and gift attached to that purchase. You can still fly the free world. Your rights under applicable consumer law continue to apply.

Privacy

The ComplianceAide operates Aerie. Your browser remembers flight preferences and your last departure. We use first-party cookies to recognize your ticket and sharing rewards. Clearing browser data can remove local progress and guest rewards.

Stripe collects payment details. If you choose wallet checkout for Google Pay, Stripe also asks for an address to calculate tax on your digital ticket. Aerie uses your checkout email and payment references to deliver tickets, send your confirmation, verify access and handle refunds. We use Microsoft Azure for hosting, access records, transactional email and crash reports. A crash report can include the world, device platform, frame rate and graphics counts to help us diagnose a failed flight.

If you submit a public post for review, we store the link and review result. For a private share, you select the recipients in your own messaging app. Aerie receives your confirmation. Support requests include the message and reply address you enter. Joining the flight-notes email list is optional.

Optional traffic analytics

If you allow analytics, Aerie remembers a random browser ID for up to 90 days to count browsers across visits. We record a general referral source, visits to the landing page, flight starts and handoffs to Stripe checkout. Aerie stores the records in its Azure account for 90 days and reports totals. The analytics records contain no email addresses, payment information or full referring links.

Analytics is optional. You can change your choice here at any time. Turning it off clears the browser’s analytics ID and stops future recording.

Contact us to ask what information we hold, request a correction or deletion, or leave the email list. We may need to verify your request and keep records required for transactions or by law. You can also read Stripe’s privacy policy.

Contact Aerie

For a charge, a gift or trouble opening your worlds, contact player support. The form gives you a case number. For privacy questions or if the form is unavailable, email info@thecomplianceaide.com. Leave out card numbers and private gift links.
