KEENE MAIN STREET PHOTOGRAPHIC TEXTURES

These local textures use perspective-corrected crops of photographs of the actual buildings. The crops have been resized and color-balanced. Selected areas of the Colonial and Exchange fronts were repaired to reduce occlusions. The Exchange and marquee textures include cutout masks. The dates below describe the photographs.
