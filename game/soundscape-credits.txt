AERIE REGIONAL SOUNDSCAPES

Field recordings placed in 3D in each world. Excerpts were trimmed, filtered below 45 Hz, level-matched and faded or loop-crossfaded. Thunder strikes and close raindrops are synthesised physically in the game. Reverberation is simulated per world with pyroomacoustics (MIT). Binaural rendering uses Omnitone 2.0 (Google, Apache-2.0; see omnitone-LICENSE.txt) with SADIE HRIRs.
