Weirs Beach and Alton Bay, Lake Winnipesaukee:
Orthoimagery from USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program, New Hampshire 2023, resampled at 0.60 m. Terrain from USGS 3D Elevation Program. Tree crown positions are approximate detections using aerial imagery and ESA WorldCover 2021. Mapping © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) with additional Microsoft US Building Footprints (ODbL). The boardwalk, station, water bandstand and waterfront structures are reconstructions.

Bowen and Stirling Falls use reprojected LINZ image tiles CB08_10000_0104 and CA08_10000_0503. Local cataract terrain combines the Copernicus land surface, mapped shorelines and river courses. The Bowen lip and basin were shaped to the documented 162 m drop and the whitewater footprint in the aerial photograph. The lower slopes at Stirling are constrained to the fjord shore and the documented drop. Tree positions near the falls are inferred from crown texture. Milford's seabed incorporates the Global Multi-Resolution Topography (GMRT) synthesis, distributed by the Marine Geoscience Data System at the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory of Columbia University under CC BY 4.0. Background cells may use the synthesis's lower-resolution bathymetry.

Weirs Beach excursion wharf
The original 3D M/S Mount Washington model depicts the modern three-deck passenger vessel moored in its summer port. The operator supplies its 230 ft length. The wharf is located on the USDA 2023 NAIP orthophoto. The hull, windows, superstructure, rigging, graphics, gangway and berth are modeled interpretations based on operator and local field views. No vessel or municipal photograph was reproduced in the model.
https://cruisenh.com/pages/our-fleet
https://cruisenh.com/pages/our-ports
https://weirsbeach.com/reasons-to-visit/attractions/cruises/
https://www.laconianh.gov/gallery.aspx?PID=3

Alton Bay Water Bandstand and Keene Central Square
The original bandstand model follows Alton's surveyed building and platform footprints, the 2019 restored exterior and subsequent photographs of the operational signal mast. The timber, low siding, name board and flags are newly modeled or drawn. Flag details and the mast position are interpreted.
https://www.alton.nh.gov/forms/planning/2022%20Master%20Plan.pdf

The United Church of Christ in Keene and Soldiers' Monument use local measured footprints. The south facade, clock and bell stages, bronze portrayal of Martin Milmore's 1871 figure, and Roxbury-granite pedestal are original models. Dimensions and visual study are from the National Register, the City of Keene, the Historical Society of Cheshire County and Keene Public Library, and a contemporary view supplied by an organization that meets at the church. Source photographs are not incorporated into these models.
