Aerie — additional landscapes

Weirs Beach and Alton Bay, Lake Winnipesaukee:
Orthoimagery from USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program, New Hampshire 2023, resampled at 0.60 m. Terrain from USGS 3D Elevation Program. Tree crown positions are approximate detections using aerial imagery and ESA WorldCover 2021. Mapping © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) with additional Microsoft US Building Footprints (ODbL). The boardwalk, station, water bandstand and waterfront structures are reconstructions.
https://imagery.geoplatform.gov/iipp/rest/services/NAIP/NAIP2023_CONUS/ImageServer
https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer
https://esa-worldcover.org/en
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://github.com/microsoft/USBuildingFootprints

Yosemite Valley, Bryce Canyon National Park and Crater Lake National Park:
Aerial photography from USDA's National Agriculture Imagery Program, delivered by USGS The National Map. Heights are resampled USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) terrain. The central 2.9 km of Bryce Amphitheater uses fins and hoodoos measured by the 2018 Southern Utah QL1 USGS survey. Its one-meter lidar elevation product was sampled at 1.42 m for the game. Trees and waterfalls are simulated.
https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer
https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer

Nā Pali Coast:
