COLORADO RIVER AT HORSESHOE BEND

Bed elevation and substrate distribution come from Matthew Kaplinski, Joseph Hazel Jr., Paul Grams, Thomas Gushue, Daniel Buscombe and Keith Kohl, USGS, 2022. The survey combines 2013 photogrammetry with 2014–2016 hydrographic work. The game uses multibeam, total-station and single-beam/interpolated soundings within the current river footprint and the concurrent published substrate classifications. NOAA National Geodetic Survey GEOID18 relates the source's GRS80 ellipsoid heights to the scene's NAVD88 stage. The nearby bank, open sky, reflected sandstone and canyon shadows retain their local illumination.

The reference view is Paul Grams's USGS photograph of Horseshoe Bend from March 16, 2012, included in the channel-mapping data release. The game renders its own three-dimensional river. Survey depth, gravel and sand areas, and broad vegetation groups constrain the reconstruction. The individual stones, plants, ripples and their motion are inferred. Regulated water level varies, and the game's stage is a fixed reconstruction.

Water motion uses eight reach-wide speed medians computed from the 10,000 cubic feet per second scenario of the USGS FaSTMECH study by Scott Wright, Matthew Kaplinski and Paul Grams, 2024. The published tables round the horizontal coordinates. The medians give the speed response to depth and the mapped game channel supplies the local downstream direction.
