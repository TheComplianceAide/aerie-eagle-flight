Moosehead Lake and Mendenhall Glacier geographic canopy layers.
Retains all existing Aerie terrain, shoreline, glacier, and surveyed trees.
The original regional scenery sources are credited in credits.txt and
mendenhall-geology-credits.txt.

The compact instance-coordinate packs use modeled canopy height and
positive optical vegetation agreement, not measurements of individual tree
locations, widths or species. Sources are resampled to the game's map frame.
Coverage gaps leave the original scenery unchanged. Runtime rechecks stock
terrain, water, creek/ice constraints and spacing; no source rasters are served.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2025).
Moosehead: Sentinel-2C Level-2A S2C_19TDL_20250816_0_L2A.
https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a/items/S2C_19TDL_20250816_0_L2A
Mendenhall: Sentinel-2B Level-2A S2B_8VNK_20250904_0_L2A.
https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a/items/S2B_8VNK_20250904_0_L2A

Exact source tile URLs, bounds, grid registration, source hashes, and
processing rules are provided in moosehead-canopy-preview.json and
alaska-canopy-preview.json.

Mendenhall optical layer: same 9 × 8.5 km native EPSG:6337
window; actual September 4, 2025 Sentinel-2 RGB at 10 m and SCL / SWIR
native 20 m reprojected to the optical grid. NDWI and NDSI weight SCL
observations, not new water or snow outlines. Fine stock ortho, reflectance,
slope/atlas shading, and reconstructed lake/Fresnel remain in the engine.
The broad optical contribution and water response are color appearance
guidance, not depth, turbidity, material chemistry, or a live freeze map.

One inland area (game x 420–550 m, z 1635–1750 m) additionally uses USGS
Southeast Alaska Landslides D22 2023 bare-earth LiDAR tiles 767733 and
768733 (native 0.5 m, resampled 1 m horizontally to EPSG:6337).
Source NAVD88 elevations retain original units; the game uses the
existing 18 m reference offset, an 18 m seam feather, and a residual
guard. Stock cove, waterfall, channels, shoreline and glacier geometry
are unaltered. This is a newly incorporated patch, not a new survey date.
https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/80000
