Acadia and south Lofoten source-bounded geographic layers.
The optical observations guide broad dry-land color, lake and fjord color
inside the existing water surfaces, and native-model vegetation candidates.
The surface boxes and source backing preserve the authored surf, reefs,
coastal terrain and buildings. Mask classes describe satellite observations
of the area on the listed date.

August 21 scene S2B_19TEK_20250821_0_L2A. RGB 10 m; spectral/SCL native 20 m.
https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a/items/S2B_19TEK_20250821_0_L2A
