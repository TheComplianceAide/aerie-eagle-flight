On the westbound Route 26 departure, the two exposed south-wall crests use a one-metre USGS 3DEP inset. The road bearing and position were estimated by fitting the visible skyline to the elevation model. Slope, convexity and orthophotography guide the rock and forest cover. The local field references guided the interpreted graphite-colored faces and the roadside guardrail. Crown estimates coincident with bare cliff returns were screened and exposed stands include narrow evergreens. The water, sky and sunlight keep the existing autumn dawn. The rock material combines CC0 photogrammetric fracture and lichen scans with the Rock Face 03 substrate listed above.

Table Rock's exposed summit spur uses a locally re-fitted half-metre ground surface from the 2015 Connecticut River public lidar point cloud. The elevation field resolves exposed near-surface returns that were omitted from the original ground classification. The prominent longitudinal joint, shallow cross-joints and lichen weathering are interpreted against New Hampshire State Parks' published site photographs. The photographs are references only. The georegistered close surface combines Marble Cliff 01 by Amal Kumar, Lichen Rock by Rico Cilliers and Rock Face by Greg Zaal and Dario Barresi, all CC0.
