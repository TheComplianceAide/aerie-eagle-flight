ACADIA NATIONAL PARK — OCEAN PATH, MOUNT DESERT ISLAND

Land elevations. U.S. Geological Survey, National Map 3D Elevation Program
(3DEP), public domain U.S. Government work. Local east/up/south coordinates
use WGS84 / UTM zone 19N (EPSG:32619), with origin at easting 564651 m,
northing 4907768 m. The 2.9 km square around Ocean Path is sampled at 2 m,
the 6.656 km square around the eastern shore at 6.5 m, and the 32 km
background at 50 m. Heights are quantized in 0.25 m steps for rendering.
The submerged seabed is a modeled surface, recessed from the wet side of
the DEM for rendering through the water. It is not a hydrographic chart.
Coastal slope normals are sampled from an additional one-metre USGS 3DEP
export across the near cliffs. Geological surface texture is rendered on
these slopes from the shared game cliff material. Moonlight and terrain
occlusion are computed in game coordinates.
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services
https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer

Aerial color. USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program imagery supplied by
the USGS National Map; U.S. Government public-domain source imagery.
Daytime orthophotographs are mapped to the surveyed ground and lit at night
by the game. Exports provide 1 m/pixel along Ocean Path, 1.625 m/pixel near
the coast, and approximately 10.4 m/pixel in the background. A small missing
blue-channel area in the close export uses the registered wider orthophoto.
https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer
https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/national-agriculture-imagery-program

Habitat. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by the
ESA WorldCover consortium. ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 10 m, CC BY 4.0. The
N42 W069 source tile was reprojected into UTM zone 19N and the published
land classes were run-length encoded. Trees and animals are game models.
https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Landmarks. USGS Geographic Names Information System feature coordinates,
layers 5 and 7, provide named anchors for Otter Cliff, Sand Beach, Great
Head, Otter Point, Gorham Mountain and The Beehive. The Thunder Hole flight
targets the visible inlet in the aerial image rather than the offshore
point registered to the hydrographic feature. Launch positions and wildlife
are arranged for the simulation. National Park Service, Hike Ocean Path,
and the park driving directions verify the order of the named locations.
https://carto.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/geonames/MapServer
https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-ocean-path-trail.htm
