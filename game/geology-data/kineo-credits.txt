MOUNT KINEO, MOOSEHEAD LAKE, MAINE

The game loads four metre-scale GLBs. The east cliff and wave margin use the original world-space survey coordinates. A stitched triangular apron connects each measured surface to the native terrain. A full-resolution index and a boundary-preserving distance index share vertices. Two detached scan analogues sit in fitted dry positions on the wave margin. Flights, landing clearance and regional loose-rock placement query the upper surface triangles.

East cliff. A 181 m long section of the eastern face of Mount Kineo reaches 183 m vertically. The major profile uses USGS land elevations. Small joints and sub-face relief are inferred, with up to 2 m horizontal offset from the sampled contour.

Wave margin. A separate 41 m long exposed strip follows the 312.23 m lake surface. Its darker low band represents wet stone. The survey terrain and model edges meet exactly in model coordinates.

Jointed block. A photographic scan supplies the form of a 1.12 m block. The base color is calibrated to the regional fine-grained rock. The original isolated stone was photographed elsewhere.

Talus pocket. A 7.84 m wide photogrammetric group provides photographed contact shapes, chips and gravel anchored near the foot of the wall. The individual stones are inferred.

## Geography and material provenance

The Maine Geological Survey describes the origin, geology and cliff setting of Mount Kineo in Circular GFL-236 (Spigel, 2018). Maine's Moosehead regional management plan and the USGS Geologic Names record identify the erosion-resistant Kineo volcanic unit. Its fine blue-gray rhyolite guides the material palette. Some nearby rocks belong to different geological units. [Maine Geological Survey](https://digitalmaine.com/mgs_publications/556/) · [Moosehead regional geology](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/parks/get_involved/planning_and_acquisition/management_plans/docs/Moosehead_Plan_1997.pdf) · [USGS geologic unit](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Geolex/Units/Kineo_2320.html)

The USGS 3DEP one-metre DEM tile USGS_1M_19_x44y507_ME_WesternMtns_B24 supplies the measured profile. Elevation sampling and both exported positions are NAD83 / UTM zone 19N. The files use glTF X east, Y up, Z south. Filtered and reconstructed surface details are recorded in each GLB. [Original USGS DEM](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/1m/Projects/ME_WesternMtns_B24/TIFF/USGS_1M_19_x44y507_ME_WesternMtns_B24.tif)
