REGIONAL MINERAL AND SHORE SPECIMENS

OpenAI ImageGen source images were separately generated from local geology, morphology and weathering descriptions. Each of the 40 sheets contains 16 labeled source identities in the manifests. Contour, visible texture and apertures come from the isolated source image. Hidden sides, scale within a size class and individual positions are reconstructed. Specimens and their deposits depict mapped units, transported rock and plausible local shore habitats. Site-specific measurements and independent licensing for the Kineo surfaces and the Mendenhall shore are in kineo-credits.txt and ../mendenhall-geology-credits.txt.
