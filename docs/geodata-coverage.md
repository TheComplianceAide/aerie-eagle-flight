# Flight-world geodata coverage

Ranges use locally registered RGB, vegetation, water and snow observations where these add useful information to the existing surveys. On the nine ranges with spectral fields, the native 10 m Sentinel-2 RGB and NIR and the native 20 m SWIR and scene classes are sampled on a 20 m grid. Cloud, cloud shadow, no-data and ambiguous pixels fall back to stock. Height-map observations influence only generated gap vegetation. The biological species and exact positions of generated plants are estimates. RGB guides the dry-ground and canopy palette while the fine orthophotography and shore materials supply local detail.

## Source terms and omissions

- Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2024, 2025 and 2026). Summer and early autumn coverage varies by site. The masks are observations of those dates and are used to choose where to blend color and model plants. [Copernicus Sentinel data access](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/sentinel-data/sentinel-2)
- Meta and World Resources Institute (WRI), 2026, Version 2 High Resolution Canopy Height Maps (CHMv2). Source imagery for CHM © 2016 Vantor. Accessed 22 November 2026. The product models approximately 1 m canopy heights from images collected on multiple dates. See each tile’s acquisition metadata for timing. The packed 20 m samples and 4 m inputs represent different levels of generalization. [CHMv2 registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/dataforgood-fb-forestsv2/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- The USGS 3DEP patches use public-domain bare-earth data. The Maine Coastal Acadia image service lists no use restrictions and credits Maine DEP, James W. Sewall and Bluesky Geospatial. [3DEP catalog](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS%3A77ae0551-c61e-4979-aedd-d797abdcde0e) · [Maine imagery rights](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/335fa5b637fa4ff0a8ff112fc5b94b7a?f=json)
- The Swiss inset is attributed to the Federal Office of Topography swisstopo. The Norwegian DTM1 remains attributed to © Kartverket under CC BY 4.0. High-resolution Norwegian orthophotos may carry Geovekst terms. The new color contribution there uses Sentinel-2. [swisstopo open terms](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/terms-of-use-free-geodata-and-geoservices) · [Kartverket terms](https://www.kartverket.no/en/api-and-data/terms-of-use) · [National DTM distribution](https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/geonorge/ATOM/hoydedata/Hoydedata_ServiceFeed.atom)
- Québec’s two exact south-narrows sheets already contribute to the game, under CC BY 4.0. Broader provincial ortho and LiDAR replacement would require its own terrain and shoreline correspondence checks. [Québec imagery](https://www.donneesquebec.ca/recherche/dataset/imagerie-orthorectifiee-du-quebec) · [Québec LiDAR products](https://www.donneesquebec.ca/recherche/dataset/produits-derives-de-base-du-lidar)
- China’s CNSA publishes a 16 m Gaofen-1 and Gaofen-6 sharing program. The research samples have narrower terms. No better redistributable scene was confirmed for an exact flight footprint. Roscosmos offers ordering through its federal data fund. No useful exact intersection with verified redistribution rights was established there. [CNSA sharing announcement](https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/n6758823/n6758838/c6808018/content.html) · [LASAC sample terms](https://www.isprs.org/news/announcements/details.aspx?ID=399) · [Roscosmos Geoportal announcement](https://niitp.ru/en/press-tsentr/novosti/novyy-geoportal-roskosmosa)

New elevation is confined to the three listed dry insets. Measured patch sources are referenced to the stock datum with no fitted offset. The runtime feathers their edges and tapers large disagreements. Fine stock wet geometry and surveyed features take precedence. Away from the measured insets, elevation and shoreline accuracy depend on the original surveys and how the game samples them.
