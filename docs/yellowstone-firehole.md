# Yellowstone · Firehole River

Yellowstone is Aerie’s thirteenth world. The playable area is a 12 × 18 km section of the Firehole watershed, from the Lone Star backcountry to the Lower Geyser Basin. The low route from Old Faithful follows the river past the Upper Geyser Basin and Biscuit Basin before climbing to reveal Grand Prismatic Spring and Excelsior.

The terrain starts with the USGS 2020 bare-earth lidar survey. Aerial orthoimagery from July 2022 supplies the forest openings, steam terraces, sinter, roads and pools. Ten detailed survey patches use imagery at approximately 0.6 m per pixel, with terrain sampled at 2 m in those patches. Forest geometry uses canopy positions and heights derived from the source point cloud, sampled for the browser. Park imagery and GPS points establish the location of each mapped departure. [Survey and asset credits](../game/yellowstone-credits.txt)

Old Faithful, Castle, Giant and Lone Star use the NPS photogrammetry meshes. Grand Prismatic, Excelsior, Morning Glory and Sapphire use outlines registered to the aerial survey. The river uses mapped bank sections and elevation profiles. Its ripples, refraction and shoreline wash follow the current. Thermal steam follows the simulated wind. Fairy Falls and Kepler Cascades use the existing falling-water and impact-spray system at the mapped drainages.

The NPS Yellow­stone Sound Library provides spatial clips of Old Faithful, Geyser Hill runoff and birds, Castle, Grand, Beehive, Anemone, Sawmill, Black Sand Pool, the Firehole and Lower Geyser Basin. Grand Prismatic supplies a separate recording from Midway. Bison and elk calls follow the animal when it is audible. [Recording manifest](../game/yellowstone-data/recordings.json)

Biscuit Basin follows the 2022 imagery. The modeled water depths and close bank deposits supplement the measured surfaces. The weather and eruption timing are authored for a clear late-summer flight.
