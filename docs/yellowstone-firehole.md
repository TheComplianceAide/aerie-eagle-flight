# Yellowstone · Firehole River

Yellowstone is Aerie’s thirteenth world. The playable area is a 12 × 18 km section of the Firehole watershed, from the Lone Star backcountry to the Lower Geyser Basin. The low route from Old Faithful follows the river past the Upper Geyser Basin and Biscuit Basin before climbing to reveal Grand Prismatic Spring and Excelsior.

The terrain starts with the USGS 2020 bare-earth lidar survey. Aerial orthoimagery from July 2022 supplies the forest openings, steam terraces, sinter, roads and pools. Ten detailed survey patches use imagery at approximately 0.6 m per pixel, with terrain sampled at 2 m. Selected low-flight sites use 3.125 m render geometry on desktop and 6.25 m on touch. Desktop loads the finest photograph at the departure and streams other high-resolution photos as the eagle approaches. Forest geometry uses canopy positions and heights sampled from the lidar, with USGS LANDFIRE 2025 vegetation type and cover guiding the tree forms and understory at 30 m. [Survey and asset credits](../game/yellowstone-credits.txt)

Old Faithful, Castle, Giant and Lone Star use the NPS photogrammetry meshes. Grand Prismatic, Excelsior, Morning Glory and Sapphire use outlines registered to the aerial survey. The Firehole uses mapped bank sections and elevations, with a modeled riverbed below the water. Wind roughens the current, and nearby banks and trees appear in the desktop reflection. Thermal steam follows the wind. When the sun is behind the eagle over Grand Prismatic, light scattered by the steam can form a glory. Fairy Falls has an airborne jet and impact spray at the mapped drainage. The Old Faithful Inn has a photo-derived roof material and dormers based on National Park Service references, and flight clearance follows the same roof and deck triangles drawn in the scene.

The NPS Yellowstone Sound Library provides spatial clips of Old Faithful, Geyser Hill runoff and birds, Castle, Grand, Beehive, Anemone, Sawmill, Black Sand Pool, the Firehole and Lower Geyser Basin. Grand Prismatic supplies a separate recording from Midway. Bison and elk calls follow visible animals. Additional NPS recordings follow specific forest, meadow, water and riparian habitats. Fairy Falls and Kepler Cascades use Aerie's shared waterfall recording, positioned at the flow's impact points. [Recording manifest](../game/yellowstone-data/recordings.json)

Biscuit Basin follows the 2022 imagery. The modeled water depths and close bank deposits supplement the measured surfaces. The weather and eruption timing are authored for a clear late-summer flight.
