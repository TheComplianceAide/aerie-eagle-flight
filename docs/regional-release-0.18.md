# Twelve-world scenery in Aerie 0.18.0

The twelve regional libraries contain at least **2,606 scenery assignments**, or **10.34 times** the audited starting inventory of 252. Every world reaches its own original tenfold target. Deduplicating shared source assets across worlds gives **2,422 demonstrated unique assets** in these categories. Establishing a globally unique total of 2,500 would require documenting another 78.

The release includes 2,368 distinct sheet-derived 3D specimens. The 46 vegetation sheets contribute 736, the 40 geology sheets contribute 640, and the 62 field sheets contribute 992. Each has a name, distinct near geometry and a native regional assignment. Across the 148 source images and the 2,368 geometries, source hashes, geometry hashes, and normalized triangle-edge fingerprints were unique. The geometry fingerprint compares normalized triangle edges after correcting for rotation, uneven stretching and shear. Names, materials, viewing angles, alternate detail levels and index order do not add identities. Three worlds already carried 624 sheet-derived forms in 0.17.0. The full promotion adds 1,744 compared with that release.
