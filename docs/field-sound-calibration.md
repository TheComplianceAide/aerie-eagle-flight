# Aerie field sound calibration

The field measurements below are airborne levels. L90 is the level exceeded for 90 percent of the observation and describes its quieter background. L50 is the median. LAeq averages A-weighted sound energy over the stated interval. The game uses differences and ranges to tune specific sources. Browser audio level is digital and physical playback level depends on the listener's hardware and volume.

| Mendenhall, trail and ridge | The Forest Service's 1999 measurements gave **42 / 32 dBA L90** at West Glacier Loop Trail and Bullard Mountain, and daytime hourly **53 / 45 dBA LAeq** with aircraft present. The nearby Antler River reached **52 dBA L90 and 53 dBA LAeq** with persistent river and falls sound. Apply it to outdoor beds and distant water carry. Near waterfall impacts retain their own level and flight wind follows airspeed. Nugget Falls, Steep Creek and the Spruce launch still lack exact calibrated readings in the reviewed record. |
| Winnipesaukee, developed shoreline | West bank of **Paugus Bay** near the Weirs Channel. A calibrated four-day July 2017 survey reported daytime and evening residual levels above **30 dBA L90**, hourly averages at or above **50 dBA LAeq**, and high-event **L01** near **70 dBA**. Excursion train horns produced the largest spikes. [City of Laconia study and plot, PDF pages 13–15](https://www.laconianh.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/1431?fileID=1589#page=13) | Use half the mixed active-to-residual difference to size the contribution of road traffic at the Weirs boardwalk. It decays with distance, terrain and darkness. Quiet Alton Bay retains the existing natural mix. Exact Alton Bay and Weirs Beach meter readings and a separate measured horn model are still missing. |
| Surry Mountain Dam and Keene | A seven-day calibrated Wheelock Park Campground baseline measured **48 dBA LAeq** and **33 dBA** for the average of each day's lowest hourly L90. Nearby highway traffic dominated. [City of Keene monitoring report, PDF pages 96–97](https://keenenh.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2021_12_20_PB_Agenda_Packet_web.pdf#page=97) | Use half the active-to-quiet difference for the localized downtown road layer. Campground data provide city context. Main Street and the dam outlet still lack matched readings. |
| Dixville Notch, low and high wind | A calibrated fall survey included the wooded trail south of the state park and the Balsams ski slope. A six-station fit gave **32 to 38 dBA L90** as normalized 10 m wind increased from **4 to 10 m/s**. [NH Site Evaluation Committee acoustic study](https://www.nhsec.nh.gov/projects/2008-04/documents/app_appendix_28.pdf#page=13) | Use the 6 dB range for rustling leaves and ambient terrain wind, centered at 7 m/s. The fit includes other nearby stations. Flume Brook and individual cliffs lack readings. |
| Horseshoe Bend, cliffs and river | Glen Canyon National Recreation Area summer natural ambient estimates were **19.1 dBA** for canyon and cliffs and **24.3 dBA** for water, based on calibrated monitoring in those habitats. [NPS Glen Canyon report, Table 8](https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/FileSource/Get/2321424?filename=GLCA_Acoustic+Report_REVISED_20130924.pdf) | Transfer the 5.2 dB contrast to the near-water mix as the eagle descends. Terrain and source distance also control audibility. These are park habitat estimates from other sites in the recreation area. |
| Moosehead Lake, sheltered woods | At Katahdin Woods and Waters station KAWW001, winter forest natural ambient estimates were **27.4 dBA day / 26.0 dBA night**. [NPS KAWW Table 6](https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/FileSource/Get/2313208?filename=KAWW_2022_AcousticMonitoringReport_FINAL.pdf#page=32) | Apply a conservative 1.4 dB day-to-night range to the background. The source is a regional winter forest proxy. Moosehead shoreline measurements remain unavailable in the reviewed record. |
| Lac Berté, boreal chalet and shore | An unoccupied chalet on Lac Knife, in another eastern Québec spruce-moss landscape, measured **37.0 dBA day / 33.7 dBA night** in October 2013. Wind in trees, waves and birds were dominant. [Québec Lac Knife assessment, Table 7.12](https://www.ree.environnement.gouv.qc.ca/dossiers/3211-16-012/3211-16-012-3.pdf) | Apply the 3.3 dB day-to-night range to the dry natural bed, interpolated using the scene's light. The rain and roof runoff have separate position and weather controls. Lac Berté exact readings remain unavailable. |
| Oeschinen Lake, chute coverage | Reichenbachfall at 44% and 156% of median discharge measured **−1.54 and +1.50 dB** relative to median discharge. [Swiss waterfall measurements](https://zenodo.org/records/5561805) · [Field methods](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021WR030980) | Apply this narrow range to the relative balance of smaller and fuller visible chutes. Chute width is a playback control. The comparator did not measure Oeschinen Lake or the game's discharge. |
| Lofoten, land wind and open surf | A Norwegian maritime heather and bog survey estimated about **26.4 dBA LAeq at 2 m/s** and **33.2 dBA at 4.5 m/s** with wind observed 2 m above ground. Gust and shelter supply the game sea-state cue. Reine harbor continues to use its sheltered sound. Hamnøy lacks verified calibrated field levels. |

## Cliff returns

The height-map renderer now finds steep walls beyond flat foreground terrain. It traces a first reflection to a finite face, checks that its height and lateral extent support the bounce, and screens both real path segments. An echo arrives after the longer travel path and from the direction of the face. Bare steep faces retain more high frequencies. Vegetation and screened paths soften the return. The manual eagle call uses the same path solver. [Image-source method](https://jontalle.web.engr.illinois.edu/Public/Allen-PDFs/PDFs/AllenBerkley79.pdf) · [Measured outdoor cliff reflections](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/175336306/Lassfolk_Rainio_Spectrum_Analysis.pdf#page=10) · [FHWA propagation and shielding](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/Environment/noise/noise_barriers/design_construction/design/design03.cfm)

Terrain and cover supply the reflection model. Individual Aerie cliff faces do not have measured impulse responses. The focused regression suite checks a roughly 280 ms return from a cliff 48 m away, an intervening ridge, the finite cliff ends and crest, and vegetation-related filtering.

Run `node tools/check_location_audio.cjs` for field-triggered gains, traffic placement, cliff return geometry and existing environment transitions. `node tools/check_directional_sound.cjs` checks terrain screening and directional playback over flight routes.
