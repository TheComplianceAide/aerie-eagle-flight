# Using Aerie

You can play Aerie, study its source, build a personal version and share a noncommercial fork. Include the applicable license texts or links and keep each creator’s notices when you share your version.

## Game code and tools

Aerie’s contributors license their original software under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md). It covers use, modifications and distribution for the purposes it specifies. The full text defines permitted personal uses and use by the listed noncommercial organizations.

## Original creative work

Aerie’s contributors license their original artwork, models, writing and documentation under [Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 International](LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt), to the extent they hold the relevant rights. When you reuse this material, credit the identified creators, link to the project and license, and identify your changes.

Commercial use of protected Aerie material, including selling a copy or a derivative game, charging for access, or monetizing gameplay through ads or in-game purchases, requires separate permission from the applicable rights holders.

## Third-party material

Upstream licenses and public-domain dedications continue to govern the portions supplied by others, including third-party source embedded in game files. These include Three.js and its Water renderer, Omnitone, pako, photographs, field recordings, terrain surveys, cartography and adapted scenery. Each specific upstream notice takes precedence for the material it identifies. Public-domain data and independently licensed assets can be obtained and reused under their own terms.
